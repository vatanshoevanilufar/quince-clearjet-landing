Where Quince is today

Growth is outrunning a shipping model built for an earlier stage

Quince's manufacturer-to-consumer model works because it removes layers — factories ship close to directly to the customer, with no warehouse or storefront markup sitting in between. That's a sourcing advantage. It puts real pressure on the parcel network, since there's no store experience to soften a late delivery or a clumsy return.

A generous 365-day return window is a strong trust signal for a brand with no fitting rooms, but it also means reverse logistics volume that needs to move as cleanly as the outbound order did. And with the assortment now spanning apparel, home, and made-to-order furniture, no single carrier profile fits everything moving through the network.
